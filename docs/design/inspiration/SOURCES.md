## Rules of thumb

- **Owner original / owner photo of own property** → safe to ship a derivative.
- **Public-domain scan (Subbuteo box art > 70 years, etc.)** → check the
  jurisdiction, but usually safe as reference. Never ship the raw scan.
- **Screenshot of a broadcast, magazine or website** → reference only. The
  agent may derive palette hexes and general motif language but must not ship
  a traced silhouette or logo built on that image.
- **Modern kit photograph** → reference only. Colours may inform the palette;
  the kit itself is not recreated in shipped UI.
